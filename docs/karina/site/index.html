The Challenge

Man-Overboard training has a fatal flaw.

MOB drills are among the most critical skills at sea — yet the tools haven't changed in decades. A standard buoy doesn't float, drag or behave like a human. Crews build muscle memory on a lie, and in a real rescue it fails.

🎯

False Muscle Memory

Train against a buoy that doesn't move like a person, and your instincts betray you when it counts.

⚖️

Wrong Weight & Drag

A real body creates unique resistance. Nothing standard replicates a 55–65 kg tow across open water.

👤

No Human Shape

Rescuers train on abstract cylinders — not the anatomical form they'll recover under pressure.

✦

KARINA Solves All Three

Life-size. Life-weight. Anatomically shaped. Head floats, body submerges. The first true MOB mannequin.

Patent & Innovation

A registered innovation.

KARINA is the first training mannequin built to reproduce how a human body behaves in a man-overboard event. It is patent registered and is a training aid — not presented as a certified life-saving appliance; no SOLAS/IMO certification is claimed.
