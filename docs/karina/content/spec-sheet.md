## ENGLISH

### KARINA
**The Ultimate Maritime Rescue Dummy**
*A life‑size, life‑weight man‑overboard (MOB) training mannequin.*

### The problem it solves
Standard MOB training buoys don't float, drag, or behave like a human being — so crews build muscle memory that fails in a real rescue. KARINA behaves like a real casualty: head floats, body submerges, realistic drift and tow.

### Technical specifications
| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Total height | 158 cm · life‑size female form |
| Dry weight | 70 kg · matches average adult female |
| In‑water weight | 55–65 kg · anatomically accurate buoyancy |
| Shoulder width | 42 cm · standard female proportions |
| Buoyancy profile | Head floats · body submerges · realistic drift |
| Construction | Waterproof fabric · internal filling · calibrated weights |
| Retrieval system | Integrated towing strap · compatible with all standard rescue gear |
| Operating environments | Ocean · port · lake · pool |
| IP status | Patent registered · prototype complete · production ready |

### Key features
- **True MOB behavior** — head above water, body submerged, realistic drift.
- **Calibrated weight** — 70 kg dry / 55–65 kg in water, anatomically distributed.
- **Professional tow strap** — integrated waist + thigh straps for realistic retrieval.
- **Open‑water ready** — ocean, port, lake, pool; built for intensive, repeated use.
- **Visual realism** — anatomical human form for psychological readiness.
- **Durable & low maintenance** — quick to dry, easy to store, reusable across hundreds of sessions.

### Who it's for
Naval & Coast Guard · Sailing Schools & Skipper Courses · Superyacht Crew Certification · Maritime Police & Rescue · Lifeguard Training Centers · Offshore & Oil‑Platform Workers · Maritime Academies · Cruise & Ferry Operators

### Important note
KARINA is a training aid. It is patent registered and is **not** presented as a certified life‑saving appliance; no maritime certification (e.g. SOLAS/IMO) is claimed.
